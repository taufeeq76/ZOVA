🛡️ ZOVA — Safer Campus. Stronger You.

ZOVA is a campus safety and harassment reporting platform designed to help students report ragging, stalking, harassment, threats, and cyberbullying confidentially while connecting them with the appropriate campus authorities and emergency support.

🎯 Problem Statement

Students may hesitate to report harassment because they fear exposure, do not know whom to contact, or are unsure whether their complaints will receive attention. ZOVA aims to simplify reporting, protect sensitive information, and support timely escalation of repeated incidents.

✨ Key Features

* 📝 Confidential Reporting — Report offline ragging and online harassment.
* 📎 Evidence Uploads — Attach relevant screenshots, photos, and supporting documents.
* 🏫 Campus Directory — Find faculty, HODs, Deans, campus security, and support contacts.
* 🚨 Emergency Assistance — Access relevant emergency and helpline contact information.
* 📊 Complaint Tracking — Monitor complaint status and updates.
* 🔄 Automatic Escalation — Route repeated related complaints to the appropriate authority.
* 👥 Role-Based Access — Provide separate permissions for students, faculty, HODs, Deans, and higher authorities.
* 🔐 Privacy and Security — Restrict access to sensitive information according to authorized permissions.

🏗️ How It Works

1. Register and enter your campus and user details.
2. Submit an offline or online harassment complaint.
3. Add incident details and supporting evidence, if available.
4. Track the complaint using its unique reference ID.
5. Authorized authorities review the complaint and take appropriate action.
6. Repeated related incidents can be escalated according to the configured escalation policy.

ZOVA is designed around confidentiality, controlled access, and responsible complaint handling. Sensitive information should only be accessible to authorized personnel. Emergency contact information should be verified, and urgent incidents should be directed to appropriate emergency services.
